Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What information is collected

To generate content, you provide business details (name, category, location, brand voice) directly into the tool. This content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response, and is not stored on our servers beyond what's needed to complete that request.

2. Access codes

Your access code and unlock status are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage), not on a central server tied to your identity.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider (Lava.top or AppSumo). We do not directly collect or store your card details.

4. What is not collected

Local Signal does not use tracking cookies, third-party ad trackers, or behavioral analytics.

5. Your rights
