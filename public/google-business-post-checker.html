FAQ

How long can a Google Business Profile post be?Up to 1,500 characters. Shorter posts of 150–300 characters usually perform better, and only the first lines show before “more”.

Why was my Google Business post rejected?Common reasons are phone numbers or links in the text, misleading or unverifiable claims, and spammy formatting like all caps. Fix those and resubmit.

How often should I post on Google Business Profile?Once a week is a good rhythm. Regular posts show customers and Google that the business is active.
